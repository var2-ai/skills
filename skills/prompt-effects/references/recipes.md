# Recipes: one prompt per effect
Each recipe has the effect sentences. For a new clip write: "{style} shot, {ratio},
{camera}. {subject} {setting}." then the effect. For an edit write the edit opening from the
skill, then the effect, then "Do not change ...". Replace every {slot}. Notes come from our
test renders.

## neon-outline
A thin glowing neon outline hugs the {subject}'s silhouette exactly and stays locked to its
edge as it moves. On {beat}, the outline hard-cuts to a new colour: {colours: hot pink,
electric blue, lime green, orange}, then repeats. No fades between colours. Only the outline
glows; the {subject} keeps its real colours.
Note: a dark or plain background makes it pop. In edits, a stray line can show for a moment.

## beat-colour-flash
On {beat}, the whole picture hard-cuts between bold two-colour duotone looks: {pairs: magenta
and navy, then yellow and black, then cyan and red}, then natural colour, and repeats. Each
look holds until the next beat, with no fades. The {subject}'s motion stays smooth through
every colour change.
Note: expect a few flashes, not every beat.

## doodles
Hand-drawn 2D marker doodles in {colours: white and yellow} draw themselves on around the
{subject}, one by one: small five-point stars, little sparkle crosses, two hearts, and a curved
arrow pointing at {the hero detail}. They wobble slightly every few frames like a hand-drawn
animation, move with the {subject} and never cover it. Flat marker lines, no glow, no text.
Note: in edits, ask for "big, bold" doodles.

## word-behind
One huge word, "{WORD}", in bold {colour} capital letters fills the {wall or sky} behind the
{subject}. The {subject} stands in front of the word and covers part of the letters; the word
is behind it, never on top of it. The letters slide up into place in the first second, then
hold still. Spell it exactly {W-O-R-D}. No other text, no logos.
Note: worked in both modes, spelled right, with the subject in front.

## stripes-behind
Thick hand-drawn vertical stripes in {two colours} stand on the {wall or sky} behind the
{subject}. They draw themselves on from top to bottom in the first second, then wobble
slightly every few frames like a hand-drawn animation. The {subject} stays in front and the
stripes are hidden wherever its body is. Flat felt-tip colour, no glow, no text.

## comic-dots (new clips only)
Everything except the {subject} is turned into a comic-book halftone pattern: big {dot colour}
dots on {ground colour}, like printed pop art. The {subject} stays real and photographic, with
a thick white sticker-cut edge around its silhouette that follows it. The dot pattern holds
steady and does not shimmer. No text, no logos.
Note: as an edit it drew a different graphic. For an existing clip, offer sunburst instead.

## scrapbook
Scrapbook collage look: the {subject} gets a thick white paper sticker edge around its
silhouette that follows it. Behind it, a torn strip of brown kraft paper runs across the frame,
with two pieces of translucent tape on its ends. The paper has a light texture and the graphics
jitter slightly like stop-motion. The {subject} stays in front of the paper strip. No text, no
logos.

## sunburst
The background is a bold retro sunburst: wide alternating {colours: orange and cream} rays
radiate out from behind the {subject} and slowly rotate. The {subject} stays real and
photographic, with a thin white cut-out edge around it, in front of the rays. Flat graphic
colours, no text, no logos.
In an edit, open the effect with "Replace the {background} behind it with a bold retro sunburst".

## photo-card
At {seconds} seconds the moment freezes into a still photo: the frozen frame shrinks into a
white-bordered instant photo card, tilts slightly and drops onto the centre of the screen with a
small bounce. Behind it the video keeps playing, softly blurred. No text on the card.
Note: freeze on the peak (the tape breaking, the first bite). Use a clip of 5 s or more. In
edits it lands late, so ask for about 1.5 s to get it near 2.5 s.

## A whole prompt (a new clip, neon-outline)
Product shot, 16:9, static camera. A white leather sneaker slowly turns on a small turntable in
a dark studio. A thin glowing neon outline hugs the sneaker's silhouette exactly and stays
locked to its edge as it turns. On a steady beat, twice a second, the outline hard-cuts to a
new colour: hot pink, electric blue, lime green, orange, then repeats. No fades between colours.
Only the outline glows; the sneaker itself keeps its real colours. No text, no logos.
